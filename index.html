Installation Guide

How to Install

Creating Bootable USB

  1. Download Rufus from rufus.ie (latest version).
  2. Insert your USB flash drive into your computer.
  3. Open Rufus and in "Boot selection", click Select and choose the Windows 7 MSB Sri Lanka.ISO file.
  4. Set Partition Scheme to MBR.
  5. Click Start and wait for Rufus to finish.
  6. Once "Ready" appears — your USB is ready! ✅

Booting from USB

  1. Insert the bootable USB into your computer while it's off.
  2. Power on and press the boot key (usually F8, F11, F12, or Del) until the BIOS/boot menu appears.
  3. Select your USB flash drive from the boot menu.
  4. Press Enter to boot from USB and begin installation.
